      Built for real masjid jamat times

      Jamat times, iqamah times, and masjid updates in one place.

      Most prayer times, salah, and namaz apps show when the prayer window begins. Takbeer Time is for the moment your local masjid actually starts jamaat: Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, and Jummah. Search nearby masjids, follow a trusted timekeeper, and keep your family on time for the first takbeer.

      For worshippers

      Find “jamat times near me,” check the next iqamah at your default masjid, and set reminders before jamaat.

      For timekeepers

      Publish accurate masjid prayer times, receive suggested corrections, and let followers know when timings change.

      For masjid websites

      Use the open API to show crowd-sourced jamaat and Jummah times on your own site or community display.

                      Time keeper ratings

                      One masjid can have several time keepers.

                      Takbeer Time chooses the highest-rated time keeper by default, so the app starts with the timings most likely to be trusted.

                      Rating uses votes, fresh updates, verification, contribution history, and keeper trust.

                      Followers show how many people use that keeper's timings.

                      To use someone else's timings, open a masjid, scroll to Time keepers, and tap "Use this time keeper's timings."

                      Delete your account?

                      This is permanent. Here's exactly what happens:

                      • Your account row (email, name, password hash, Firebase ID) is removed.
                      • Your favourites, default masjid, preferred keepers, and reminder preferences are deleted.
                      • Suggestions you've sent to time-keepers are deleted.
                      • Submitted jamat timings stay in the directory but are re-attributed as "Deleted user," because other users are relying on them right now.
                      • Your push-notification subscriptions on this device are dropped.
                      • You'll be signed out and the page will reload.

                      If you want specific submissions removed too, email qazi.junaid@gmail.com.
